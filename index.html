I am an Automation and AI Systems Engineer currently working for a media company in Germany. I build the systems that run their daily operations automatically, without anyone having to do things manually.

Think of what I do like this: instead of a person checking emails, copying data into a spreadsheet, and sending a WhatsApp message every day, I build one system that does all of that on its own, every single day.

Based in Nairobi, Kenya. Working with clients globally. I hold a BSc in Mathematics and Computer Science and certifications in AWS Cloud, Google Cloud Security, and AI Development.
