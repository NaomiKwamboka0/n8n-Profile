Who I am

Systems that work
while you sleep.

I'm an automation and AI engineer based in Nairobi, Kenya. I work with companies to build systems that handle daily tasks automatically while keeping their data secure. Think of it this way: instead of one person checking emails, copying data into a spreadsheet, and texting customers every single day, I build one system that does all of it on its own.

Right now, I work remotely for Rushlake Media GmbH. But I also help clients all over the world. I have a degree in Mathematics and Computer Science, plus certifications in Cloud security and AI.

When you work with me, you get systems that are secure, smart, reliable, and easy to manage. No more manual work. Just systems that work.
